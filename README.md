One line: the diagnosis an AI therapist gives its human, funny enough to repeat out loud. A house judge model reads the lines two at a time and keeps the funnier one. Entry for make.fun's "Your AI Therapist's Diagnosis" bounty, $2 in USDC.

The diagnosis:

> Diagnosis: seasonal gift-card hoarding. You own eleven gift cards and are saving them for a special occasion that is death.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the single line (123 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.
